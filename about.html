Since 1998

Small enough to know you.
Big enough to surprise you.

Roselyn began with a simple idea: education works best when every student is seen, supported, and encouraged to think beyond the expected. Nearly three decades later, that idea still guides everything we do.

Today, our students learn across disciplines, work side by side with faculty, and take their ideas into the community. We make room for exploration—and help you turn what you discover into something meaningful.
